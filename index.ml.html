HOW TO PLAY

1. Select a game mode and press NEW MATCH.
2. Move the striker left or right along your baseline.
3. Touch the striker and pull backwards to aim.
4. Release to shoot the coins into the pockets.
5. Pocket your coins to score points.
6. Pocket the Queen and then another coin to cover it.
7. Avoid pocketing the striker: it is a foul.
